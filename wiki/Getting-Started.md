# Getting Started (testnet, five minutes)

1. **Get the wallet** from [the releases page](https://github.com/kdsmith18542/LuckyLode/releases) and
   install it (Linux AppImage).
2. **Make an address** in the wallet (Receive tab) and copy it.
3. **Get play money** from the [Faucet](Faucet) (10 tLKYL per address per day),
   or skip straight to mining — payouts need no starting balance.
4. **Mine:** wallet Mining tab → GPU/CPU → Start. Or CLI:
   `lm-miner pool --pool luckylode.cc:3346 --worker YOUR_ADDRESS`.
5. **Watch:** your worker appears on https://luckylode.cc/pool/ within a
   minute; payouts land automatically every block to your address.

No account, no password, no pool signup. Your address *is* your identity:
payouts, streaks and league rank all key off it. Guard the wallet seed
like real money — testnet resets can wipe chain balances, but your keys
are yours (see [Testnet Rules](Testnet-Rules)).
