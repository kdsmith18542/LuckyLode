# Testnet Rules

1. Coins are **worthless**. No value, no promises, no trading.
2. The chain **may reset** with notice. Back up your wallet seed and
   any Irreplaceable labels — chain balances are not guaranteed.
3. **Back up your seed.** Testnet or not, lost keys are lost coins.
4. Report breaks with height + txid + what you expected. The point is
   breaking things before mainnet.
5. Don't mine on the VPS/seed box — mine from your own hardware against
   the public pool or solo (see [Pool Mining](Pool-Mining)).
6. Faucet abuse (drip farming across generated addresses) just burns
   the shared reserve faster for everyone. Don't.
7. Consensus rules are frozen for the testnet run; game tuning
   (divisors, cadence) is announced before it changes.
