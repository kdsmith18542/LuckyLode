# Faucet

Free testnet coins: https://luckylode.cc/faucet/

- **10 tLKYL** per address per day. Paste any testnet address, drip.
- Funded by the consensus faucet reserve (seeded at genesis, topped up
  from per-block claims) — the page shows the live reserve, never
  topped up by hand while it lasts.
- One drip per address per day, tracked server-side (429 + next time
  while cooling down).
- Drips are normal transactions — confirmed within a block or two,
  spendable immediately.
- Testnet coins have no value and the chain may reset (see
  [Testnet Rules](Testnet-Rules)). Don't test with anything you can't lose.
